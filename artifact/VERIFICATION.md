## Method

1. **Writing.** Each item has a clinical scenario, four distinct options, one keyed answer, a
   rationale, and a one-line reason for each wrong option. Options are shuffled per item at build
   time; the key falls on A/B/C/D 282/268/249/278 times.
2. **Blind review.** Each domain's questions were exported with the key, rationale and reasons
   removed and the options shuffled, then answered cold by a separate reviewer agent that had
   never seen the key. The reviewer gave its answer, named any second defensible option, and
   recorded any factual concern. Answers were compared with the key by script.
3. **Source check.** Guideline families that changed recently were checked by web search:
   AHA CPR/ECC 2025, AHA/ASA acute ischemic stroke 2026, Surviving Sepsis Campaign 2026,
   ADA hyperglycemic crises 2024, ACOG Rh immune globulin 2024, ESI v5, ABA burn resuscitation,
   and BCEN's own exam facts. Most confirmation came from secondary summaries, not primary text.
4. **Option-length audit.** The keyed option was the longest in 66% of items as first written.
   One distractor was lengthened in 433 items so that this now happens in 25.6% (chance).
5. **Re-review.** All 439 items whose stem or options changed in steps 3-4 went through a second
   blind review.

## Limits of this verification

- The blind reviewer is the same underlying model as the author, in a fresh context without the
  key. It catches ambiguous keys and many dated facts; it shares the author's blind spots.
- Not every dose and lab value in 1,077 items was traced to a source. Only the guideline families
  listed above were checked, mostly through secondary summaries.
- No item has been reviewed by a nurse or pilot-tested with candidates.

## Board versus outline

Found while checking the topic list; none of this was changed except the passing standard.

- **Passing standard.** BCEN lists 99 of 150 for exams on or after 6 July 2026. The board said 106,
  the previous standard. Changed to 99.
- **Domain structure.** The outline has no "Trauma & Multisystem Shock" section. It has
  "Musculoskeletal and Wound Emergencies" (11 items), with shock split across cardiovascular,
  neurological and medical. Communicable diseases sit with environment and toxicology (13 items).
- **Outline subtopics with no topic of their own on the board.** These are covered inside the
  nearest topic's questions: croup, pleural effusion, pulmonary hypertension, inhalation injury,

  resuscitation, placental abruption and previa, hyperemesis, ovarian disorders, urinary
  retention, osteomyelitis, amputation, injection injuries, Bell's palsy, glaucoma, retinal
  detachment, communicable diseases, cultural humility, abuse and neglect, impaired nurse and
  drug diversion.
